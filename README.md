# UsefulTrophiesAgain
Allows the player to consume trophy items to gain random stats
Amount of xp gained is configurable, along with the option to disable boss trophy consumption

#### Credits
This mod is a fork and adaptation of the great work done by [Khairex](https://github.com/Khairex) on their original mod [UsefulTrophies](https://github.com/Khairex/ValheimMods). Given that Khairex has seemingly fallen out of activity in the Valheim community and left their mod to grow incompatible with the newer versions of the game, I have taken the liberty to fork and re-release the mod under a new name.

Special Thanks to [Khairex](https://github.com/Khairex) for their original work!
